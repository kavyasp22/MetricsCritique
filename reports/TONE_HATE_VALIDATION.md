## Caveats

- One labeller (the assistant), blind to scores, not confirmed by a human. Treat every figure as provisional until a human reviewer checks at least a random subset.
- Neutral-versus-opinion and hate-versus-insult boundaries are judgement calls; a different labeller could move the figures.
- The text scored is `post_facts.text`: the translation where one exists, otherwise the original. Original-language text was not compared.
- Language and platform cells are small (6 to 12 posts). They indicate where to label more, not measured accuracy.
- Hate precision is re-weighted by band size from stratified samples. The top band has only 30 sampled posts.
